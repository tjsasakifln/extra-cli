# B2G X-Ray — observed public portfolio

Input: normalized 14-digit CNPJ from the visitor, or an authorized canonical
entity id.

Output: observed contracts, organs, UFs, typologies, sample concentration,
documented events, comparables only when defensable, related analyses, and
second-read **candidate refs**.

Not emitted: risk score, credit, pain, irregularity, capacity, consent, or
total market share without a denominator.
